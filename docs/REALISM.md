# Synthetic data realism report

Generated by `python -m matchlens.calibrate.fit` on 2026-10-09 (params `fit-skillcorner-4aeb62071c`, 1307 s).

**Reference:** SkillCorner Open Data (github.com/SkillCorner/opendata), MIT licence. A-League 2024/25: 20 matches of dynamic events, plus season physical aggregates for 153 outfield players. Raw files are downloaded at fit time and never committed. This report and `fitted_params.json` contain aggregates only.

**Method:** the simulator stays fully synthetic (a fictional league, seeded). Calibration only tunes its parameters by coordinate search, minimising squared relative error on the targets below. Event parameters are fitted on 12 seeds; physical parameters on 3 seeds with 10 Hz tracking. Numbers below are on **held-out seeds** that were never used in fitting.

Definitions follow the source where possible: passes are player possessions ending in a pass; a sprint is ≥ 25.2 km/h; peak speed is the 99.9th percentile of 1 s speeds per player (PSV-99 analogue).

Known gaps: event definitions are not one-to-one with the source (for example, SkillCorner on-ball engagements vs our pressure events), and team shape is rule-based rather than learned.
