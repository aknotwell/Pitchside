# Notices

## Data used for calibration only

MatchLens never ships or streams real match data. Every match is generated by
our own simulator in a fictional league. The simulator's parameters were
**fitted** to aggregate statistics from:

- **SkillCorner Open Data**, A-League 2024/25 (<https://github.com/SkillCorner/opendata>),
  MIT licence, © SkillCorner. Used: dynamic event files for 20 matches and
  season physical and passing aggregates.

Raw files are downloaded at calibration time into `data/raw/` (gitignored) and
are not redistributed. The repository contains only fitted parameters and
aggregate statistics: `src/matchlens/sim/fitted_params.json` and
`docs/REALISM.md`.

## Trademarks

All clubs, players, kits and the "Northern Shield League" are fictional. No
league, club or broadcaster marks are used. MatchLens is not affiliated with
any league or club.
